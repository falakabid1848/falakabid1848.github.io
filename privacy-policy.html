2. Information Collection and Use

WhatsToolKit is engineered to operate primarily as a local client-side tool with minimal data collection:

  • On-Device Contacts Permission: Our app allows you to select recipients from your address book using Android's system contact picker or local selection sheet. Your contacts are processed solely on your device in real-time to facilitate messaging. We NEVER upload, sync, or sell your contacts to any server.
  • User-Initiated Registration & Cloud Sync: If you voluntarily choose to sync your profile with our developer Web Store, we securely store only the full name and WhatsApp contact number you provide via encrypted HTTPS connection to our Firebase Realtime Database.
  • Media & Photos: Status Saver and media attachment features access your device's local storage solely to read WhatsApp status files or attach documents you choose. All files remain strictly on your local device.

🔒 Data Security Pledge: We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information or address book data to any third party. All communication with our servers is secured via 256-bit TLS/SSL encryption.
